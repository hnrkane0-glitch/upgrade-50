🌐 Custom Domain

Already own a domain? Link it so customers visit your store at your own address.
Don't include https:// or any path. Subdomains like shop.yourbrand.com work too.

How it works

  1. Type your domain above and press Connect Domain.
  2. Log in where you bought the domain (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Whogohost, Qservers…) and add the DNS record shown here.
  3. Wait a few minutes (sometimes up to a few hours), then press Check Connection.
  4. When it shows Connected, your domain opens your store and that becomes the link to share.

Pricing & Currency

🛒 Checkout Options — how customers pay on your store

Choose which payment choices customers see at checkout. Turn on Pay Now so they can enter their card and pay immediately, and/or Pay on Delivery.

💳 Moniepoint & OPay card payments (no Paystack needed)

Customers enter their card on Moniepoint's or OPay's own secure page and the money is paid into your account there. You need a merchant / business account with them (a normal personal OPay wallet or POS account can't take online card payments). Your keys are stored encrypted on the server and can never be seen by customers or other stores.

🟦 Moniepoint (Monnify)
Sign up at monnify.com (Moniepoint's payment gateway) → Developers → API Keys & Contracts. Copy the API Key, Secret Key and Contract Code.
🟩 OPay
Create an OPay merchant account (OPay Business / Cashier) → Settings → API Keys & Webhooks. Copy the Merchant ID, Public Key and Secret Key.

After saving, tick the provider in Checkout Options above and press Save checkout options. Keys already saved are never shown again — leave a box empty to keep the saved value.

💳 Checkout Payments — your own Paystack & bank

Customers who buy from your store pay with their card through your Paystack account, so the money is settled to the bank account on your Paystack dashboard. These details belong only to you — no other store owner can see or use them.

🔐 Payment details saved & locked
Get it at dashboard.paystack.com → Settings → API Keys. Only the public key (pk_…) goes here. Your secret key goes in POS → Settings → Payment terminals (stored encrypted on the server).
💡 The bank account is shown to customers who choose Bank transfer at checkout and at your POS. Card payments settle to the bank linked on your Paystack account.
